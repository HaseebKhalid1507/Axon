# Hopper W4A8 round 2: the upper-bound probe says KILL (2026-10-26)

Roadmap item 2 ("Hopper exact GEMM, round 2"), track T3 of pick-20261026. This was a 2-day **upper-bound probe**, not
the build. It answers one question: how fast can a warp-specialized TMA + RS-wgmma Q4_K × Q8_K kernel go **with no
exact epilogue at all**? It is deliberately inexact, so it bounds every exact kernel built on this structure. The
probe code is test-only, on branch `feat/t3-w4a8-probe` (PR #150, closed unmerged). Nothing reaches a product path.

Labels: **MEASURED** (H100 job below), **ESTIMATED** (arithmetic shown), **OPINION**.

## Pre-registered rule (written in NOTES.md and the #150 body before any H100 timing)

- Gate cell: `probe_w4a8_n64`, Q4_K K 3584 × rows 18944, nb 1024, H100 SXM, median of 9 interleaved rounds, TMAC/s
  of real MACs. The cell's integer bit check must pass in the same job.
- **< 240 TMAC/s → shelve round 2.** Why 240: the exact per-256 drain costs ≥ 25 % of issue (research
  hopper-exact-w4a8 §3.2), and 240 × 0.75 = 180, the roadmap's bar. **≥ 240** → report and stop; the 6–10-day
  exact build would need the lead's go.
- Only the n64 tile gates. It is the largest tile whose registers can also hold the exact kernel's fp32 accumulator.

## What the probe is (CODE, `src/cuda/probe_w4a8.cu` on the branch)

- **CTA and roles:** 384 threads, one CTA per SM, a persistent grid of #SMs, tiles of 128 rows × 64 tokens, tokens
  fastest.
  - **Producer warpgroup** (setmaxnreg 40): issues the TMA loads per stage (raw Q4_K rows {144 B, 128}, activations
    2 × {128 B, 64} SW128). Three warps build the SW128 tile of activation 32-sum pieces for the mins term.
  - **Two consumer warpgroups** (setmaxnreg 232): RS `wgmma.m64n64k32.u8.s8`. They unpack Q4_K into u8 registers
    (4 + 2 scale split) in 4 rotating A slots, one commit group per k32 step, and `wait_group<3>` before reusing a
    slot.
- **Per 256-block:** 18 wgmma (8 lo + 8 hi + 2 for M = Σ m·bsum on the tensor core). That is all the tensor work an
  exact kernel needs.
- **Accumulation:** S and M accumulate in int32 over the whole K and drain once per tile. There is no d / dmin / dk
  and no fp32 chain.
- **Pipeline and resources:** 4-stage ring, 168 KB smem. NVRTC 13.4 sm_90a gives 168 registers, 0 spills, and no
  wgmma serialization note.
- **Checks:** every probe cell is bit-checked against a CPU integer reference (S, M; 4096 sampled outputs + tile
  corners per cell; every output on five uneven shapes). That reference is itself checked against `quant::dequant`.

## Verdict

**224.8 < 240: KILL. Round 2 of the Hopper exact GEMM is shelved, per the pre-registered rule.** It is recorded in
[experiments-rejected](../experiments-rejected.md). The roadmap's item 2 is marked shelved. The roadmap's own text
makes item 4 (FP8 under `--numerics fast`) the replacement path, and that path needs Haseeb's decision on the
determinism-modes ADR. It has not been started.

## How the session went (and what broke)

- **Run 1** (job `1026-213102-2581`, pod2, **$1.60** per the pod ledger, incl. ~12 min idle from an axon-run placement bug, fixed
  separately): the peaks ran, then the first probe launch died with `CUDA_ERROR_MISALIGNED_ADDRESS`.
  - **Cause, found by reading:** the producer loaded the activation's 32-value sums with `int4` (16 B) loads. Those
    sums sit at 2·np + np/64 bytes into each activation (decode.cu `act_view`), so they are only 4-byte aligned:
    +516 B at np 256, +7224 B at np 3584.
  - **Fix:** scalar loads (056094c).
  - Every alignment the probe relies on is now asserted on the host before each launch (`probe_w4a8_layout`): TMA
    bases and strides 16 B, SW128 smem 1024 B, the raw TMA destination 128 B, mbarriers 8 B, the smem total. A no-GPU
    test pins the .cu layout constants and forbids vector loads through that pointer.
- **Harness restructured:** each stage runs in its own child process (peak → tiny under memcheck → tiny → uneven bits
  → bench), with a baselines-only fallback bench if the probe fails. One faulting cell can no longer lose the
  baselines.
- **The first rehearsal (job 1026-220134-e435) failed on the sanitizer, not the probe.** The PyPI
  `nvidia-cuda-sanitizer-api` wheel lacks `TreeLauncherSubreaper`, so it can't start a target ("terminated before first
  instrumented API call"). The NVIDIA redist archive 13.2.76 (sha256-checked) works.
  - The session now self-checks the sanitizer on a test that really launches kernels, and drops the stage if the
    sanitizer can't instrument Axon.
  - Off sm_90a, the probe-under-memcheck step reports "skipped (no sm_90a)".
  - Rehearsal job 1026-221735-3a70 passed on bella.
- **Run 2** (job `1026-222543-3f73`, pod2 11 min, **$0.80**; warm 159 s, the job itself 14 s): clean.
- **T3 total:** ≈ $2.40 of H100 (≈ 0.60 pod-hours, pod ledger: $1.60 + $0.80) against the $6 / 1.5 pod-hour cap, plus about 1 builder-day.

## OPINION, for the owner

The structure alone (warp specialization, TMA, an overlapped RS unpack, M on the tensor core, a persistent grid) gave
1.80–2.04×, ~1.9× over gemmh2 on every shape (raw, no exact epilogue). With the brief's ≥ 25 % epilogue cost, an
exact kernel built on it would land at **≤ ~169 TMAC/s (ESTIMATED: 224.8 × 0.75)** at the gate cell, ≤ ~1.45× gemmh2, below the
roadmap's 180 bar. This lines up with the M6 lesson: the brief's model said 240–330, and the measured upper bound
came in below its lower edge. Any smaller exact-structure follow-up (for example "take the ~1.45×") would need its
own proposal and its own pre-registered rule, not a re-reading of this one.
