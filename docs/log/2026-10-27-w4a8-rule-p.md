## The rule (verbatim from #165, fixed before the run)

- S_shape = gemmh2 ms / exact-W4A8 ms per shape, where exact = probe × (measured epilogue factor from the
  variant). Q6_K down counts at its measured ratio if the skeleton runs it, else 1. k/v and misc count at 1.
- S_weighted = 1 / (0.52/S_gate_up + 0.14/S_down4 + 0.15/S_down6 + 0.13/S_qwo + 0.05/1). These are the anatomy's
  nb 512 shares (#157). They sum to 0.99 as registered and were used verbatim.
- **GO** if S_weighted ≥ 1.34 at nb 512 *and* at nb 384; **KILL** otherwise. The bar is not lowered after the data.
- How the job read it (posted on the r10 forum thread before the run):
  - "gemmh2 ms" is the serve's in-situ kernel for each shape: `gemmh2_q4_k_gu` (gate_up), `gemmh2_q4_k` (down Q4_K,
    q/wo), and `gemm2s_q6k8` / `gemm2_q6k8` for down Q6_K. gemmh2 has no Q6_K path; batch.rs picks between the two
    gemm2 kernels by nb and SM count: gemm2s at 384, gemm2 at 512 on 132 SMs.
  - f = skeleton ms / exact ms on gate_up at nb 512, applied to every shape at both nb.
  - A cell that fails its bit check does not count. A missing number gives NO-DATA, not a verdict.

## What ran (CODE, `src/cuda/probe_w4a8.cu`, `src/cuda/qwen2/tests.rs` on the branch, dd1e7fa)

- **Four test-only kernels on #150's structure.** CTA of 384 threads: one TMA producer warpgroup and two RS-wgmma
  consumer warpgroups. One CTA per SM, persistent, tiles of 128 rows × 64 tokens.
  - `probe_w4a8_n64`: the Q4_K skeleton. Integers over all of K, with no drain; unchanged from #150.
  - `probe_w4a8x_n64`: Q4_K **exact**. The same tensor work plus the per-256 drain, in gemmh2_q4_k's float ops and
    order: `v = d·S − dmin·M`, then `acc = fma(dk, v, acc)` block after block.
  - `probe_w6a8_n64`: a Q6_K skeleton on a probe copy ("PW6": the Q6K8 values, plus the 16 int8 scales as three
    balanced radix-8 digit planes, d ∈ [−3, 4]).
    - One IMAD per 32-bit word builds the A bytes `q·d + 128` ∈ [0, 252], so a u8.s8 RS wgmma takes them.
    - The +128 term is per token, not per row: `S_b = P0 + 8 P1 + 64 P2 − 9344 Σx_b`.
    - That is 3 tensor passes per k32 step, against Q4_K's 2.25.
  - `probe_w6a8x_n64`: Q6_K **exact**, `acc = fma(dk·dw, float(S_b), acc)` as gemm2_q6k8 does.
- **Shapes:** Qwen2.5-7B's, with synthetic weights (random blocks with sane f16 scales):
  - gate_up: Q4_K, K 3584, 2 × 18,944 rows. The probe runs on the stacked 37,888-row matrix.
  - down4: Q4_K, K 18,944, 3584 rows.
  - down6: Q6_K, K 18,944, 3584 rows.
  - q/wo: Q4_K, 3584 × 3584.
- **Timing:** nb 384 and 512, 7 rounds, base / skeleton / exact interleaved in one round with the order rotated per
  round, 3 timed launches after a warm-up, medians.
- **Session:** one job, child processes, stage budget 660 s:
  - NVRTC sm_90a compile + ptxas -v (0 spills required);
  - a self-test of the rule's arithmetic on fixed numbers;
  - the wgmma peak;
  - compute-sanitizer memcheck on the four tiny launches;
  - bits per kernel on ragged shapes (every output);
  - the bench, with only the kernels whose bits passed.

```
RULE P INFO nb 512: S_weighted from the exact variant measured on every shape 1.5430 (not the rule's number)
RULE P VERDICT: GO
```

- **Verdict: GO** (1.5420 / 1.5722 ≥ 1.34). Measured on every shape directly, the exact variant gives 1.5030 /
  1.5430: also a GO, by a margin of 0.16–0.20.
- **The epilogue costs 16 %, not 25 %.** f = 0.8358 (MEASURED) vs the 0.75 the brief assumed. The two consumer
  warpgroups hide part of each other's per-256 drain.
- **The exact kernel is bit-identical to today's kernels on every output** (MEASURED, ragged shapes and every timed
  cell).
  - A production path built on it keeps the default contract (identity C in `docs/design/determinism-modes.md`).
  - It changes no numerics. It is a faster route to the same bits as gemmh2_q4_k / gemm2(s)_q6k8.
- **Q6_K is the weak shape** (1.11× / 1.21× exact). The three digit planes put 33 % more tensor work per block than
  Q4_K's 2.25 passes (3 / 2.25). At nb 384 the probe's 28 × 6 = 168 tiles on 132 SMs also leave a 36-tile second
  wave (ESTIMATED from the grid; the persistent loop does not remove a tail).
- **Against the predictions:**
  - the pick's prior was S_weighted ≈ 1.26–1.30 (KILL);
  - my pre-run estimate was 1.42 / 1.44, with f ≈ 0.78;
  - the measurement beat both. My skeleton ratios held (about 2.0× estimated, 1.96–2.04× measured on Q4_K);
    the miss was f (0.836 measured vs my 0.78 guess).

## What didn't work / what this does not show

- **These are isolated launches on synthetic weights, not a serve pass.** The end-to-end effect is ESTIMATED in the
  design note and needs its own pre-registered A/B.
- **gate_up's exact variant stops before silu·up.** It is compared with gemmh2_q4_k on the stacked matrix, i.e.
  gemmh2_q4_k_gu's values before that step. The fused epilogue runs once per output and isn't in the timed cell.
- **The mode-1 (residual add) and bias epilogues that wo / down use in the serve are not in the probe.**
- **Pass sizes below 384 were not measured in this job.** The 10-26 probe had nb 128 (skeleton 1.68× gemmh2 on gate).
- **Not covered:** k/v (512 rows) and other quant types.
