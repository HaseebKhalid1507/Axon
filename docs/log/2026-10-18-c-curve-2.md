# C curve 2: q|k|v and gate|up in one gemvm launch at nb ≤ 16  (PR #86)

- Date: 2026-10-18. Branch: feat/c-curve-2 (built on #81, merged as 0dbf9fd; main merged in at 6f7901a). Status: open
- Pick: the nb ≤ 16 term of r12-perf-pick Pick 1, which #81 left untouched (T_step(16) 1.60× vs the gate's ≤ 1.4×).

## What changed
- **gemvmx_\*** (prefill.cu): gemvm over two or three matrices of one type and width in one launch. Each matrix keeps
  its own rows, bias and output, and block b runs matrix j's block b − (the blocks of the matrices before it). That is
  exactly the block its own launch would run (`gemvm_t` now takes the block index as an argument), so the outputs and
  bits are the same.
- **`CudaQwen2::bgemm_x`** (batch.rs): at ≤ 16 tokens, a layer's q, k, v go in one launch. If v's type differs (the 7B's
  Q6_K v layers), q and k go in one launch and v separately. Gate and up go in one launch, except with the up
  product's swiglu epilogue (`swiglu_epi`, off by default), which reads gate's output. `AXON_GEMVMX=0` turns it off.
- `pool_step_cost`: `AXON_STEP_X=1,0,…` toggles it in turn within one run.
- Profile segments: in the fused paths, `pass_at`'s `on(name)` events fold q (and v when fused) into the `k` segment,
  and gate into `gate_up`. A per-segment profile of a fused step therefore shows `k` ≈ q+k(+v) and `gate` ≈ 0. Compare
  totals, or run with `AXON_GEMVMX=0`, to split them.

## Why
The decode step's own profile (`cuda_profile`, 7B, position 600, RTX 4090) against the pool's step at nb 1 (same
weights, same position) showed where the batched path's extra ~1.1 ms goes:

| | decode step | pool step nb 1 |
|---|---|---|
| q / k / v | 0.45 ms (one fused launch) | 0.91 (three launches; k and v are 512 rows = 32 CTAs on 128 SMs) |
| gate / up | 2.42 (fused) | 2.76 (two launches) |
| down | 1.61 | 1.87 |
| head | 0.48 | 0.59 |

The pool path's small matrices can't fill the GPU on their own, and each launch pays its own ramp and tail.

## What didn't work
- **Two blocks of gemvm prefetch (`GVM_PF2`) and more resident blocks (`GVM_MINB=4`)**, aimed at gemvm16's 540–800 GB/s
  (job 1018-034145-34d7, 7B, ms per step, interleaved by build):

  | build | nb 1 | nb 8 | nb 16 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | plain | 6.50 | 7.15 | 8.70 |
  | GVM_PF2 | 6.88 | 7.67 | 9.46 |
  | GVM_MINB=4 | 6.80 | 7.22 | 10.01 |
  | both | 7.27 | 8.09 | 10.66 |

  More registers in flight cost more occupancy than they bought back. The defines were removed after measuring; the
  numbers are here and in docs/experiments-rejected.md.
- **Gate|up on gemm2s_q4_k_gu at nb 9–16** (r13 pick 1, branch feat/c-curve-3, `AXON_GEMVM_GU_MIN`): REJECTED.
  - Bench: `cuda_gu_bench` on the 7B gate|up shape, weights cycled from DRAM, gemm2s_q4_k_gu time ÷ fused gemvmx time.

    | card | job | nb 4 | 8 | 9 | 12 | 16 |
    |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
    | bella, sm_120 | 1018-060449-b71d | 1.63 | 1.54 | 1.44 | 1.35 | 1.16 |
    | bella, second pass | 1018-060449-b71d | 1.65 | 1.56 | 1.43 | 1.36 | 1.15 |
    | avante, RTX 4050 sm_89 | 1018-060449-ca07 | 1.09 | 1.11 | 1.11 | 1.12 | 1.19 |

  - Step: bella's 7B pool step at nb 16 went from 21.32 / 21.43 ms (gemvmx) to 21.93 / 22.05 (gemm2), +0.6 ms.
  - Verdict: gemm2 is slower at every nb ≤ 16 on both cards, so the kill rule (≥ 12 % faster, ≥ 0.3 ms step) fails.
  - Why the premise was wrong: it compared gemm2 at nb 32 (2.83 ms) with gemvm16 at nb 16 (3.4 ms, before #86 fused
    gate|up). gemm2's cost isn't flat below its 64-token tile.
  - The 4090 was not measured (pods out of budget). Retry only if a 4090 run shows gemm2 ahead at nb 16. The code stays
    on feat/c-curve-3 only.
- Still open for T_step(16) ≤ 1.4× (7B, nb 16, per-kernel at 600):
  - gate/up 3.4 ms and down 2.2 ms: gemvm16 at ~620 GB/s vs the decode GEMV's 884, compute-bound by NF=2's per-block work.
  - Attention at 0.94 ms vs ~0.55 for its KV bytes.
  - The per-row rope/kv stores.
