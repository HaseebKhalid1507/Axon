## What changed
- **`src/cuda/w4a8.cu`** (new, in KERNELS after hopper.cu; it compiles to nothing off sm_90a). Three entry points,
  all from #165's measured exact probe:
  - `w4a8_q4_k`: modes 0 / 1 and bias, as gemmh2_q4_k;
  - `w4a8_q4_k_gu`: silu(gate) · up, as gemmh2_q4_k_gu;
  - `w4a8_q6`: the Q6K8 prefill copy, as gemm2(s)_q6k8.
  - **Structure:** persistent, warp-specialized, TMA, RS wgmma u8 × s8, with a per-256 drain in today's float ops
    and order.
  - **Q6:** the design note's option (B). The producer's builder warps compute each scale's balanced radix-8 digits
    from the copy's own scales, so the 288-B layout is unchanged.
- **`src/cuda/qwen2/batch.rs`:**
  - `w4a8_route`: AXON_W4A8=1 on an sm_90a module, **feed passes only**, ≥ 3584 rows, nb ≥ the product's
    threshold (`AXON_W4A8_T`, ≥ 33; default 384 until P1's sweep sets it). The pass kind comes from a guard the feed
    callers set (`feed_tokens`, `prefill_tokens`, the pool's chunk feed), so decode steps (`Place::Rows`, any nb)
    and verify passes never take it.
  - The bgemm branch (plain Q4_K, Q6) and the gu-site branch.
  - Launch counters per entry point (`w4a8_hits`); `AXON_W4A8_STATS=1` prints them.
- **`src/cuda/qwen2/slots.rs`:** one guard line. **`src/cuda/qwen2.rs`:** one AtomicBool field.
  **`src/cuda/mod.rs`:** the KERNELS list.
- **Tests (`src/cuda/qwen2/tests.rs`):**
  - CPU: `w4a8_layout_pinned`, `w4a8_routing`;
  - no GPU: `nvrtc_w4a8_compiles_sm90a` (ptxas -v, 0 spills required, unique symbol);
  - sm_90a: `gpu_w4a8_tiny`, `gpu_w4a8_matches_reference_bits` (vs gemv_ref and today's kernels, every output);
  - ignored: `cuda_w4a8_checkpoint1` (P1's bench), the 7B hit-counter tests, `cuda_w4a8_p1_session`.

## Why
Rule P (#165): the exact W4A8 skeleton × the measured epilogue factor gives S_weighted 1.5420 / 1.5722 (nb 384 /
512) against gemmh2 and gemm2(s)_q6k8, with bits identical. The design note's ESTIMATE is +13.5 % aggregate at mix
C = 128 on an H100, kill bar +8 %.
