# `--help` and the code's doc pointers made current (#222)

- Date: 2026-11-12. Branch: dec/help-docs. Base: main 213465a. Status: open
- Pick / design: #222 and its comment from #223; the two doc comments recorded on #217; the foreman's decisions brief
  (11-12, week 1 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md)).

## What changed
- `axon --help` (`src/cli.rs`):
  - `--cache-ram` no longer says an exclusive tier is pending, or that 6G buys almost nothing: the tier is exclusive
    since #201 (it drops its copies of pages the GPU pool still holds first), so its useful capacity is about its size.
    It also says where it is off (a CPU/GPU split, several GPUs, MoE).
  - The footer lists every architecture the loader takes: qwen2, qwen3, llama, gemma3, and the mixture-of-experts
    models qwen3moe, deepseek2 and gpt-oss (experts on the CPU under `--device cuda`, `--gpu-experts` for qwen3moe and
    deepseek2). It also says FP8 checkpoints run on the GPU (Q8_0 at load, or natively with `--numerics fast`).
  - The header says "GGUF language models", not "Qwen2/Qwen3/Llama 3 GGUF models".
  - The two README pointers (`--parallel`, `--slot-idle-ms`) now point at `docs/serving.md`.
- `src/lib.rs`: the crate doc's README pointers go to `docs/models.md` and `docs/environment.md`, and `serve`'s to
  `docs/serving.md` (#223 moved that text out of the README).
- `src/model.rs` (`conv_tolerance`) and `src/safetensors_tests.rs` (`fp8_conversion_need_of_the_checkpoint`): the
  conversion check refuses caps through 735 MiB, not "719–743", and about 742–745 MiB is still killed converting
  (#217, the arena's page tables).
- New test `cli::tests::help_is_current`: `--help` names the exclusive tier, the seven architectures and native FP8
  on the GPU, and no longer says "pending" or "README".

## Why
#222: `--help` was out of date since #201 (the tier) and #190 (FP8 on the GPU), and the footer had never listed
gemma3 or the MoE models. #223 moved the README's reference into `docs/`, leaving five pointers in the code dangling.

the `--help` output of main against the candidate (the diff is in the PR body). No timing: text only.
